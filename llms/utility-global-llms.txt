# Utility Global

> Utility Global is a Houston, Texas industrial decarbonization company founded in 2018. Its
> H2Gen / eXERO platform converts water into high-purity hydrogen using the residual
> electrochemical energy already present in industrial off-gases and biogenic gases — driving
> water electrolysis without electricity — while concentrating CO2 into a separate,
> capture-ready stream.

## What this company is

Utility Global is a hardware and process-technology business, not a software vendor. It sells
into steel, low-carbon fuels, and refining / petrochemicals / chemicals, and has demonstrated
hydrogen production from blast-furnace off-gas at commercial steel facilities. There is no
developer program, no API documentation, no SDK, no CLI, and no machine-readable contract of
any kind on any host the company operates.
